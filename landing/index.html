    The problem

    Five tools and an afternoon of copy and paste.

    One blog post with a video means searching, writing, editing, uploading and emailing — by hand, one tab at a time. When a step fails, nobody notices until the end.

    How it works

    Build the chain once. Run it whenever you need it.

    No code, no scripts. If you can describe your process in a sentence, you can draw it.

    1. 01

      Drag

      Pick the agents you need from the palette and drop them on the canvas.

    2. 02

      Connect

      Draw a line from one agent to the next. Isnad refuses connections that can't work, and tells you why.

    3. 03

      Configure

      Click an agent to set its topic, length, voice or destination. Every form builds itself from the agent.

    4. 04

      Run

      Each agent lights up as it works and hands its result to the next. Leave the page — it keeps going.

    5. 05

      Approve

      Before anything is published or sent, you preview it and decide.

    The agents

    Six agents. Each does one job well.

    Every agent takes a checked input and returns a checked output, so the next one always gets what it expects.

    Create

    Make the content.

    Researcher

    Searches the web on your topic and gathers notes with their sources.

    in
    topic, num_sources
    out
    notes[], sources[]

    Writer

    Turns the notes into an article in the length, style and format you choose.

    in
    notes, length, style, format
    out
    article_md, title, summary

    Image

    Creates a thumbnail and images to go with the article.

    in
    prompt or article title
    out
    image files

    Video

    Narrates the script and assembles slides into a playable MP4.

    in
    script, voice, resolution
    out
    mp4, duration

    Distribute

    Send it out — always after your approval.

    Needs approval

    Publisher

    Uploads to YouTube or Google Drive with your title, description and tags.

    in
    file, platform, metadata
    out
    remote_url, platform_id
    Needs approval

    Email

    Sends the links and files to your recipient list through Gmail.

    in
    recipients, subject, body, links
    out
    message_id, sent_at

    See every handoff

    Color means state.

    The canvas stays black and white until something happens. When a step turns a color, it's telling you something — always with an icon and a word, never color alone.

    • Pending

      Waiting for the step before it.

    • Running

      Working right now.

    • Retrying 2/3

      Something failed. Trying again on its own.

    • Needs approval

      Waiting for you before publishing or sending.

    • Done

      Finished, output checked and saved.

    • Failed

      Couldn't finish after 3 retries. The run stops and says why.

    • Skipped

      Never ran, because an earlier step failed.

    When a step fails, Isnad waits a little longer each time.

    Retries back off exponentially — 2^attempt + jitter seconds — so a busy service gets room to recover. After the third retry, the run halts and every step after it is marked skipped.

    Human in the loop

    Nothing goes public without you.

    Publishing and email steps stop and wait. You see the article, watch the video and check exactly where it's going — then decide.

    • Preview the real thingThe same article, video and PDF that will be uploaded — not a summary.
    • See the destinationPlatform, account, visibility and title, before a single byte leaves.
    • Every decision is recordedWho approved or rejected, when, and why — kept with the run.
